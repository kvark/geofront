# Tactical readability pass

The dusk city and Angel silhouettes establish an identity already. At the
current low battle-camera angle, the large glowing fixture meshes can
compete with limb and grid readability. Replacing them with original,
low-profile relay fixtures keeps the sodium/cool-neon split without
requiring environment rework. Palette aside, do not make the scenery a new
tactical rule: these fixtures are static, non-colliding landmarks.

The more important combat gap is order feedback. The Attack button previously
appeared ready for dead and out-of-range targets; an out-of-range order could
also consume a pending pilot response before the range check. The HUD should
show distance/range and warn when the target's AT Field will absorb an
otherwise legal strike. The HUD and simulation share validation, while pilot
refusal remains an intentional uncertainty for legal orders only.

## References and limits

- [Into the Breach (Subset Games)](https://www.subsetgames.com/itb.html):
  make the impending consequence legible before the player confirms.
  Geofront's limb choices and stress/sync system remain its own; this pass
  does not promise a full enemy-intent forecast.
- [Massive Assault retrospective (Wargaming)](https://wargaming.com/en/news/timeless_series_massive_assault/):
  use readable roles and a small, intelligible order flow. Geofront does
  not need more unit types to improve this moment.

No reference-game assets were copied. This is a focused iteration, not a
replacement for all placeholder mech and city art.
